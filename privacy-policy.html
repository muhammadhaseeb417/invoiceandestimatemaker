1. Scope and who we are

This policy covers the Invoice and Estimate Maker mobile app and privacy-related support communications. The app is designed for freelancers, contractors, consultants, agencies, and small businesses. It can be used without creating an account with MH Publishers.

When this policy page is viewed through GitHub Pages, GitHub provides the website hosting and may process technical request data under its Privacy Statement. That hosting is separate from the app’s local business records.

2. Information the app handles

Information you enter or select

Depending on the features you use, information stored in the app can include:

  • Business details such as a name, email address, phone number, postal address, website, tax identifier, currency, payment terms, and bank-transfer instructions.
  • Client details such as names, company names, email addresses, phone numbers, billing or shipping addresses, tax identifiers, and notes.
  • Invoice, estimate, catalog, recurring-invoice, and payment records, including descriptions, quantities, prices, taxes, discounts, due dates, payment status, amounts, and outstanding balances.
  • Expense details, including vendor, amount, payment method, and description.
  • Images you choose for a business logo or signature. Drawn signatures are created on the device. The app does not use these images to identify you biometrically.
  • App preferences such as language, currency, and selected templates.

Some of these records can contain personal or financial information about you or other people. You control what you enter. If you add another person’s information, you are responsible for having the authority and providing any notices required by law.

Support messages

If you email us or submit a privacy request, we receive the information you include, such as your email address, message, and any attachments. Please do not send passwords, full payment-card numbers, or documents that are not needed to address your request.

Information processed by advertising services

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent tools. When ads are requested or displayed, Google may process information such as your IP address, advertising or app/device identifiers, app and device details, ad request and placement information, ad impressions or interactions, and diagnostic or fraud-prevention information. An IP address may be used to estimate general location. The app does not request your precise GPS location for advertising.

We do not put invoice, client, payment, expense, logo, or signature content into ad requests. We do not currently include a separate app analytics, crash-reporting, social-login, or AI-processing SDK in the app.

3. On-device storage, backup, and deletion

The app’s business records and selected images are stored in app-specific storage on your device. We do not routinely receive or upload these records to servers operated by MH Publishers. The app does not require you to create an online account.

The Android app permits Android backup (allowBackup is enabled). Depending on your Android version, device manufacturer, Google account, and backup settings, Android may back up some app data to your Google account or transfer it to another device. Those backups are controlled by Android and Google, not by MH Publishers. Check your device’s backup settings if you do not want app data included.

You can edit or delete records within the app. Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it normally removes its local data from that device. A separate Android or Google backup, or a file you previously exported or shared, may remain under the settings and retention practices of the relevant provider or recipient. The app cannot remotely delete those copies.

CSV and JSON exports, invoice or estimate PDFs, and shared receipts are created when you request them. The Android share or print interface lets you choose another app or destination. Once sent or saved, the file is handled by that destination and its privacy practices.

4. How information is used

Information stored in the app is used on your device to create, calculate, display, print, export, and share business documents and records, and to remember your app preferences. Information sent to us is used to answer support or privacy requests and maintain related correspondence.

Google processes advertising information to provide and measure ads, help prevent fraud and abuse, and, where permitted by your consent, region, and settings, personalize advertising. Google determines its own processing purposes and retention practices for information it receives through its services.

The app does not accept online payments from you, provide user-to-user accounts or messaging, send push notifications, create user profiles for automated decisions, or provide AI-based services. You can record payments made by your customers in the app, but the app does not process those payments or collect payment-card credentials for them.

Legal basis where required

Where privacy laws require a legal basis, we rely on providing the app functions you choose to use, our legitimate interests in responding to support requests and protecting the service, consent for advertising processing where consent is required, and compliance with legal obligations. Google determines the legal bases for its own processing. You may withdraw consent for advertising through the privacy choices described below where available.

5. Advertising, consent, and identifiers

Ads are provided through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), not Google AdSense. Depending on the ad request, Google’s configuration, your region, consent choices, and device settings, ads may be contextual or personalized. Google may use mobile advertising identifiers and similar technologies; a native mobile app does not use browser cookies in the same way as a website.

Where required, the app uses Google UMP to present and update advertising consent messages and to provide an in-app privacy-options entry point when one is required. If that option is available for your region, find it under More → Privacy & Data → Advertising privacy choices. You can also review Google’s information and controls here:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google advertising and privacy
  • Google My Ad Center

Google may use information under its own privacy policy and applicable settings. We do not receive a copy of all information Google processes through the advertising SDK.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information for money, and we do not share your local invoice or client records with advertisers. The app does transmit advertising-related information to Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may use or disclose information to its service providers or advertising partners as described in Google’s policies. Some privacy laws define “sale” or “sharing” broadly; depending on your location and ad configuration, this ad-related processing may be treated as sharing, targeted advertising, or another regulated disclosure even when we do not sell information for money.

Information may also leave your device when:

  • You choose an app or destination in Android’s share, email, or print interface. The receiving service or person then handles the file.
  • Android backup or device-transfer features back up or transfer app data under your device and account settings.
  • You contact us or submit a privacy request. Your email provider or the privacy-request form provider processes the message needed to deliver it to us.

No separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK is included in the app code reviewed for this policy. Google AdMob account settings may enable advertising demand or mediation partners; any such partners process information under the applicable Google ad configuration and their own terms. Play Console disclosures must be kept aligned with the actual AdMob configuration.

7. Retention

  • App records and images: remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app, subject to any separate operating-system backup or transfer described above.
  • Exports and shared files: remain with the apps, services, devices, or people to which you save or send them. We cannot control their copies or deletion.
  • Support and privacy correspondence: is retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain necessary records, and meet applicable legal requirements.
  • Advertising information: is retained by Google according to Google’s applicable policies and settings.

8. Security

App records are stored in app-specific storage protected by the mobile operating system’s application sandbox. The app does not add a separate encryption layer to its local database. Protect your device with a passcode, keep its software up to date, and use care when exporting or sharing documents. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. International processing

MH Publishers is based in Lahore, Pakistan. Most app records stay on your device. If Android backup is enabled, if you share a file with another service, or if the app requests an ad, the relevant provider may process information in countries different from yours. Those providers’ own privacy terms and safeguards apply to their processing.

10. Children

The app is designed for business use and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 18. The app has no user-account registration. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us in a support message, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.
